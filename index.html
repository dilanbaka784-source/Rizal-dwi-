My First Experience of
Building a Website
A personal account of learning HTML & CSS from scratch — full of bugs, breakthroughs, and a lot of patience.
■
■ ■ Orientation The Beginning

Last semester, I had the opportunity to build my very first website for a school project. My teacher assigned us to create a digital portfolio to showcase our assignments. At first, I was incredibly nervous — I had no background in coding or web design at all. But I was also genuinely excited to try something new! 01

■
■ Events The Process

I started with research and chose HTML & CSS from scratch to really understand how things worked. My first win: getting 'Hello World' to show in the browser. Small, but it felt massive.

"I once spent two full hours just trying to center a navigation bar. One missing semicolon could break the whole page!"

But online forums and a lot of patience got me through it. By the end of the week, I had added project images, a short bio, and a working contact form. 02

■
■ Reorientation The Takeaway

Seeing my website live in the browser for the first time was an incredible feeling. Beyond HTML and CSS, this experience taught me something far more valuable — patience and persistence. Every bug is just a puzzle waiting to be solved. 03
